### Fixed

- **Range strip follows melee reach again.** The strip no longer says Melee at every distance: it takes melee reach from the game's own swing range, the check that dims the Main Hand timer, so walking away lights Dead zone, Auto Shot or Too far as you go.
- **Range strip never shows a wrong band.** It lights a band only when the game's range answers leave no doubt. Out of combat the game does not say whether you are in melee reach, so close in it may show no band; Auto Shot, your spells and Too far still light at range.
