### Fixed

- **Range strip follows melee reach again.** The strip no longer says Melee at every distance: it takes melee reach from the game's own swing range, the check that dims the Main Hand timer, so walking away lights Dead zone, Auto Shot or Too far as you go.
- **Range strip lights out of combat.** The game answers melee reach only in combat, and that missing answer used to hide every band. Now it hides only the bands it could change: Auto Shot and Too far light out of combat, and Melee and the Dead zone light once the game can answer.
